GVP College of Engineering, Madhurawada

Quality technical education, research and innovation in Visakhapatnam.

Gayatri Vidya Parishad College of Engineering (GVPCE), commonly known as GVP College of Engineering, is an autonomous engineering college located in Madhurawada, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. The college was established in 1996 with the aim of providing quality technical education and promoting research and innovation.

Programmes

The college offers a wide range of B.Tech, M.Tech and MCA programmes. Its undergraduate programmes include Computer Science and Engineering, CSE specialisations such as Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Data Science and Cyber Security, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Chemical Engineering and Information Technology.

Academic Recognition

GVPCE has received significant academic recognition. According to the college's official information, it has been accredited by NAAC with an A++ grade for seven years in its third cycle, and its B.Tech programmes have received NBA accreditation at various times. The institution is also autonomous and affiliated with Andhra University.

Placements & Industry

The college also has a strong focus on placements and industry connections. For the 2024–25 placement year, the college reports 1,346 placements, with recruiters including Cisco, Amazon, TCS, Infosys, HSBC, Zoho, Micron and others.

Overview

Overall, GVP College of Engineering is one of the established engineering institutions in Visakhapatnam, particularly known for its long history, autonomous academic system, engineering programmes, accreditation and placement opportunities.
